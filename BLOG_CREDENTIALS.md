## Blog Editor - Admin Credentials

### How to Use:

1. **Access the blog:** Navigate to `/blog`
2. **Sign In:** Click "Editor Sign In" button at the top right
3. **Enter credentials:** Use the email and password above
4. **Create a post:** Click "Create Post" and fill in:
   - Post Title
   - Author Name
   - Excerpt (short description)
   - Featured Image (upload a file)
   - Full Content
5. **Publish:** Click "Publish Post" - your post appears instantly on the blog!

### Social Media Sharing:

Each published post includes one-click sharing to:
- Twitter
- Facebook
- LinkedIn

Click the "Share Post" button on any blog card to open social media sharing links.

### Managing Posts:

In the editor, you can see all your posts in the "Your Posts" section on the right and delete them if needed.

**Note:** Posts are stored locally in your browser. They will persist across sessions on the same device/browser.
